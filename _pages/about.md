I am currently undergoing an undergraduate degree in Cognitive Neuroscience and Psychology, with a particular interest in the relationship between the brain, behaviour, and human performance. My studies have developed my understanding of cognition, motivation, decision-making, and the psychological factors that influence performance. I am particularly interested in applying this knowledge within performance psychology to help individuals better understand and optimise their mental approach to performance.
